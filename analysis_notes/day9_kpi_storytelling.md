KPI 1 — Total Revenue

Definition
Total monetary value generated across all recorded transactions.

Insight (Story)
Total revenue shows steady accumulation driven primarily by a limited number of higher-value transactions rather than broad-based volume growth.

Business Implication
Revenue performance is sensitive to changes in transaction size rather than transaction count, indicating potential volatility if large contributors decline.

KPI 2 — High-Value Transaction Revenue Share (≥ 200)

Definition
Percentage of total revenue generated from transactions with an amount equal to or greater than 200.

Insight (Story)
A significant share of revenue originates from high-value transactions, suggesting that a small subset of transactions disproportionately contributes to overall performance.

Business Implication
While high-value transactions boost revenue efficiency, reliance on them increases concentration risk and may require targeted retention or risk-mitigation strategies.

KPI 3 — Revenue by Category (Crypto Focus)

Definition
Total revenue aggregated by transaction category, ranked by contribution.

Insight (Story)
Crypto-related transactions represent the dominant revenue category, outperforming others by a clear margin.

Business Implication
Strong category concentration highlights an opportunity for specialization and growth, but also exposes the business to sector-specific volatility and regulatory risk.




Analyst Note

These KPIs collectively provide visibility into revenue scale, quality, and concentration, enabling informed decisions around growth strategy, diversification, and risk management.
